2. What stays on your phone

The following data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us and we have no access to it:

  • your answers to the app's welcome questions (how long you have been together, what you are looking for);
  • your favorite questions;
  • your settings (language, time of the question of the day);
  • the question packs you have already downloaded.

The "question of the day" is a notification scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

Deleting the app erases all of this data.
