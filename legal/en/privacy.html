Nousa is a questions app for couples. It was designed to collect as little data as possible: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Hugo Delseaux EI, 14 allée du Grand Chêne, 31120 Portet-sur-Garonne, France. Contact: nousa@hugodelseaux.fr.

2. What stays on your phone

The following data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us and we have no access to it:

  • your answers to the app's welcome questions (how long you have been together, what you are looking for);
  • your favorite questions;
  • your settings (language, time of the question of the day);
  • the question packs you have already downloaded.

The "question of the day" is a notification scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

Deleting the app erases all of this data.

3. What leaves your phone

Service Data Why Legal basis
Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) Purchase, payment, your Apple or Google account information To collect the subscription payment. We never see your payment details, your name or your email address Performance of a contract
RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) Anonymous ID generated by the app, purchase history, device model and operating system version, country, language, IP address To check that your subscription is active and restore it on a new device Performance of a contract
Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) IP address, when a new pack is downloaded To host the monthly question packs Legitimate interest (providing the subscription content)
Us, if you write to us Your email address and your message To reply to you Legitimate interest

Apple and Google process your data under their own privacy policies, as independent data controllers. RevenueCat and Cloudflare act on our behalf.

4. Transfers outside the European Union

RevenueCat and Cloudflare are located in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider participates in it or, failing that, by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites (the app does not ask for Apple's tracking permission).
  • No analytics or behavioral analysis tools.
  • No profiling or automated decision-making.

6. How long we keep data

Data Retention period
Subscription data held by RevenueCat As long as the subscription exists, then up to 3 years after it ends, unless a longer period is required by law
Cloudflare technical logs According to Cloudflare's policy, usually a few weeks
Support emails 3 years after the last exchange

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase or port your data, to restrict its processing, or object to it, by writing to nousa@hugodelseaux.fr.

We do not know your name or your email address: if needed, we will ask you by email for the information we need to find your subscription.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority, www.cnil.fr) or with the data protection authority of your country.

8. Minimum age

Nousa is not intended for children under 15 and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date. If the change is significant, the app will let you know.
