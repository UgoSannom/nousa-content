2. What the app offers

Nousa offers questions and games for two people to play together on a single phone: a core set of questions sorted by category, the "Which one of us?" and "Would you rather" modes, a question of the day and a new question pack every month.

Nousa is entertainment. The app is not a substitute for support from a professional (couples counselor, psychologist, therapist).
